privacy.

Last updated
3 November 2026

unblurt has no servers and no accounts. Your text goes from your computer to the AI model you chose, and nowhere else. We never see it, store it or sell it.

This policy covers the unblurt desktop app and this website (unblurt.me). unblurt is an open-source project made by Viral Sachde. Every claim below can be checked in the source code on GitHub.

The desktop app

The text you rewrite. When you press the shortcut, unblurt reads the text you selected (and any note you add) and sends it to the opencode engine running on your own computer at 127.0.0.1. opencode then sends it to the model provider you connected, such as Anthropic, OpenAI or Google, or to a model running locally on your machine.

  • unblurt does not run a server, so your text is never sent to us.
  • Each rewrite uses a temporary opencode session that is deleted as soon as you get your text back. Sessions left over from a crash are deleted the next time unblurt starts.
  • unblurt sets up its engine with sharing and automatic updates turned off, and with every tool disabled, so the model can only return text.

Your model provider. Whatever you send to a cloud model is handled under that provider's own terms and privacy policy, using your own account with them. If you use a local model, your text never leaves your computer.

Your clipboard. To read your selection and paste the result, unblurt presses copy and paste for you. It reads the clipboard briefly, then puts back what was there. After a paste, the rewritten text stays on your clipboard.

Settings. Your chosen mode, tone, model and shortcut are saved on your computer only. The engine's configuration file lives in unblurt's app data folder.

Permissions. On macOS, unblurt asks for Accessibility access so it can press copy and paste for you. It uses that permission for nothing else.

No tracking. The app has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no advertising. It does not create an account or identify you in any way.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels. The site is plain HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes standard request data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the site. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Fonts. The Inter typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when you open a page. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Downloads and GitHub. Installers and the source code are hosted on GitHub, which has its own privacy statement.

What we share or sell

Nothing. We don't collect personal data, so there is nothing to share, sell or hand over.

Children

unblurt is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the release notes on GitHub.

Contact
